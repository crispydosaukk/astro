Vāstu Alignment as a Remedy
When space is aligned, life flows with less resistance.
Vāstu is not merely about structure—it is about creating harmony between space and life.When your surroundings are aligned with natural directional energies, it supports clarity, stability, and prosperity.
दिशासु सम्यग्विन्यस्ते वास्तु शान्तिः प्रजायते ।तस्मात् सुसंस्थिते देशे सुखं समृद्धिर्विवर्धते ॥

📖 Transliteration
Diśāsu samyag-vinyaste vāstu śāntiḥ prajāyateTasmāt susaṁsthite deśe sukhaṁ samṛddhir vivardhate

📖 Meaning
“When space is properly aligned in all directions, harmony arises.In a well-balanced environment, happiness and prosperity naturally grow.”
